License Information

A driving license is an official document that certifies an individual's authorization to operate one or more types of motorized vehicles on a public road. It is issued by the Regional Transport Office (RTO) after verifying the applicant's eligibility and conducting necessary tests.

License Validity
  • Non-Transport License: Valid for 20 years or until the age of 50, whichever is earlier.
  • Transport License: Valid for 3 years and needs to be renewed periodically.
License Types
  • LMV: Light Motor Vehicle (cars, jeeps, etc.)
  • MCWG: Motorcycle With Gear
  • MCWOG: Motorcycle Without Gear
  • HMV: Heavy Motor Vehicle (trucks, buses, etc.)
Common Questions

You can apply for renewal up to 1 year before the expiry date. Click on the "Renew" button next to your expired license or apply for a new license with the "Renewal" option.

Apply for a duplicate license by selecting the "Duplicate" option in the application form. You will need to submit an FIR report for lost licenses.

You will need to apply for a new license with the additional category. You may need to undergo testing for the new vehicle category.
