Processing Guidelines
Document Verification
  • Verify all uploaded documents are clear and legible
  • Check that owner details match with ID proofs
  • Validate insurance documents are current and authentic
  • Ensure chassis and engine numbers match with invoice
  • Verify emission certificate if applicable
Inspection Scheduling
  • Schedule inspections at least 24 hours in advance
  • Assign inspections based on vehicle type and class
  • Notify applicant via email and SMS
  • Coordinate with inspection officers
Inspection Results
  • Record detailed inspection notes
  • Upload photographs of the vehicle if available
  • Verify chassis and engine numbers physically
  • Check for modifications against manufacturer specifications
  • Test all safety features as per vehicle class
Registration Issuance
  • Generate registration number from the system
  • Issue RC details with accurate expiry dates
  • Calculate and collect proper road tax and fees
  • Update centralized vehicle database
  • Notify applicant of successful registration
