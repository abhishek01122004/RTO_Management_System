Registration Guidelines
Important Information
  • All fields marked with * are mandatory
  • Documents must be clear and readable
  • Registration fee will be collected after submission
  • Processing time is typically 5-7 working days
Required Documents
  • Vehicle purchase invoice/bill
  • Valid insurance certificate
  • Pollution certificate (if applicable)
  • Clear photograph of the vehicle
Process Timeline
  1. Submit registration and documents
  2. Pay the registration fee
  3. Document verification by RTO
  4. Vehicle inspection (if required)
  5. Registration number assignment
