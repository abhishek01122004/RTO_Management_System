About Vehicle Categories

Vehicle categories define the classification of vehicles for registration purposes. Each category has its own registration fee and requirements.

Common vehicle categories include:

  • Two-wheelers: Motorcycles, scooters, mopeds
  • Light Motor Vehicles: Cars, jeeps, SUVs
  • Commercial Vehicles: Taxis, delivery vehicles
  • Heavy Vehicles: Trucks, buses, construction vehicles
  • Special Vehicles: Emergency vehicles, agricultural vehicles

Note: Deleting a category that is associated with existing vehicle registrations is not recommended.
