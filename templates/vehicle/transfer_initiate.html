Transfer Process
  1. Initiate Transfer
    Submit required documents and new owner details
    Now
  2. New Owner Confirmation
    New owner receives notification and confirms acceptance
  3. RTO Verification
    RTO officials verify documents and approve transfer
  4. Payment of Transfer Fee
    Transfer fees to be paid by the new owner
  5. Ownership Updated
    Ownership records updated in the RTO database
Required Documents
  • Sale Deed / Transfer Agreement Signed by both parties
  • Delivery Note Confirming vehicle handover
  • Form 29 (Application for Transfer) Generated automatically in the system
  • Form 30 (Report of Transfer) Generated automatically in the system
  • No Objection Certificate (if applicable) If vehicle is registered in another state
