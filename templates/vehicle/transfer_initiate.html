Transfer Guidelines
Important Information
  • Transfer of ownership must be reported within 14 days of the sale
  • Both the seller and buyer are responsible for ensuring the transfer is properly registered
  • Failure to transfer ownership may result in legal liability for future violations
Required Documents
  • Sale Deed / Agreement: Document proving the sale transaction
  • Delivery Note: Signed receipt confirming physical transfer of the vehicle
  • NOC: No Objection Certificate if the vehicle is financed/leased
Process Timeline
  1. Submit transfer application with required documents
  2. Pay the transfer fee
  3. Document verification by RTO
  4. Approval and update of ownership records
  5. Issuance of new Registration Certificate
Note: Any pending fines, taxes, or legal issues must be resolved before transferring ownership.
