Application Guidelines
Important Information
  • All fields marked with * are mandatory
  • Documents must be clear and readable
  • The application fee will be collected after submission
  • Processing time is typically 7-10 working days
Required Documents
  • Passport-sized photo (recent)
  • Signature (scanned)
  • Identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport)
  • Address proof (not older than 3 months)
Process Timeline
  1. Submit application and documents
  2. Pay the application fee
  3. Document verification by RTO
  4. Schedule driving test (if applicable)
  5. License issuance (if approved)
